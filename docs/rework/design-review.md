# GEHU visual reference review

Reviewed the official homepage, Dehradun landing page and Student Area on 20 October 2026. Scrolled through the campus cards, institutional text columns, article/event grids, notices, service links and footers, beyond the hero/video.

Sources: https://gehu.ac.in/ ; https://gehu.ac.in/dehradun/ ; https://gehu.ac.in/dehradun/student-area/

Observed patterns: charcoal masthead and footer; white navigation text on dark surfaces; consistently aligned content widths; clear equal-column grids; generous vertical spacing; plain dark headings; gray body text. The Dehradun Student Area uses Manrope and computed body color #6b6b6b. Following the user’s latest review, QuickSlot uses a shorter light frosted header and footer, a pale cool background, white glass cards, darker body text and restrained green controls. Doodles, gradients and decorative colored text are removed. Room cards are compact and distributed across the content width. The black university wordmark comes from the observed official Dehradun site asset.
